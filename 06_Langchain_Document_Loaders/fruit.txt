In gardens bright and orchards fair,
The fruits of nature scent the air.
A golden mango, soft and sweet,
Holds summer sunshine in its meat.

Red apples gleam on branches high,
Like blushing stars against the sky.
Bananas hang in crescent rows,
A moonlit smile the jungle knows.

The berries dance in colored hues—
Blue, black, and red with morning dews.
Oranges burst with zest and cheer,
Their citrus kiss both bold and clear.

A pear, so shy with gentle grace,
Holds autumn’s whisper in its face.
Grapes in clusters, purple, green,
Are nature's jewels in tangled sheen.

Pineapples wear a crown so proud,
With tropic thunder in each cloud.
From fig to plum, from peach to lime,
Fruits sing the earth’s sweet song in rhyme.

So let us taste, and feel delight,
In every bite, the world feels right.
